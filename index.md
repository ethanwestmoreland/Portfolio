
# Computer Science ePortfolio

## Professional Self-Assessment

Throughout my time in the Computer Science program at Southern New Hampshire University, I have developed the knowledge and skills needed to begin a career in software development. The program has helped me improve my programming abilities, problem-solving skills, and understanding of how different technologies work together. Through coursework and hands-on projects, I have gained experience with software engineering, databases, algorithms, cybersecurity, and application development. Creating this ePortfolio has given me the opportunity to look back at my previous work, recognize how much I have improved, and demonstrate the skills I can bring to a professional environment.

One important skill I developed throughout the program is the ability to communicate and collaborate with others. In software development, working as a team requires clear communication, organization, and an understanding of different perspectives. Through discussion assignments, project planning, and code reviews, I learned how to explain technical concepts and provide useful feedback. I also learned the importance of writing clear documentation and comments so that other developers can understand and maintain my code. These skills are valuable when working with other developers and making decisions about software projects.

My coursework also helped me build a stronger understanding of data structures, algorithms, and problem-solving. I learned how to approach programming challenges by breaking larger problems into smaller and more manageable ones. In courses involving algorithms and artificial intelligence, I gained experience with different approaches to solving problems and evaluating their efficiency. For example, in CS 370, I worked with reinforcement learning and neural networks, which helped me understand how computers can learn from data and make decisions. These experiences taught me to consider performance, efficiency, and the advantages and disadvantages of different solutions when developing software.

Software engineering and database development have also been important parts of my education. Throughout the program, I worked with several programming languages and technologies, including Java, Python, C, C++, JavaScript, SQL, and MongoDB. I gained experience designing applications, organizing code, developing user interfaces, and managing stored information. For example, in CS 340, I worked with MongoDB and created a dashboard that could retrieve and display information from a database. Projects like this helped me understand how front-end applications, back-end systems, and databases work together to create functional software. I also learned the importance of testing applications, maintaining consistent coding standards, and designing programs that can be improved over time.

Another important area of my education has been software security. Through courses such as CS 305, I learned about common security vulnerabilities and the importance of protecting applications and user information. I gained experience reviewing software for potential weaknesses and understanding how secure coding practices can reduce risks. I also learned about authentication, data validation, and protecting sensitive information. These experiences helped me develop a security-focused mindset and understand why security should be considered throughout the development process rather than added after an application is completed.

My ePortfolio demonstrates these skills through three enhancements involving two applications I previously developed. The first enhancement focuses on software design and engineering through my WeightTrack Android application. This project demonstrates my ability to improve an existing application by making changes to its structure, functionality, and maintainability. The second and third enhancements focus on algorithms, data structures, and databases through my Travlr web application. These enhancements demonstrate my ability to improve how an application retrieves and organizes information, handles user requests, and communicates with a database. Together, these projects show my experience with both mobile and web development, as well as my ability to evaluate existing software and make meaningful improvements.

Completing these enhancements also helped me understand the importance of reviewing and improving existing code. Rather than creating entirely new applications, I had to examine my previous work, identify weaknesses, and determine how to improve it. This process required problem-solving, research, testing, and careful planning. It also showed me how much my programming skills have developed since I originally created these projects. Features and improvements that once seemed difficult became easier to understand as I applied the knowledge I gained throughout the program.
